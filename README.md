# 🏠 Embedded Systems Project: Smart Home Using Yolo-bit (based on ESP32)

## 📖 Description
The **SmartHome system** enhances convenience, safety, and energy efficiency in households and offices.  
It integrates advanced technologies to **monitor, control, and automate home devices**, allowing users to easily manage them **remotely via a mobile app or web-based platform**.

---

## 👥 Team Size
- **5 members** (including 1 Computer Engineering)
---

## 🔑 Key Features

### 🚨 Fire Alarm and Fire Fighting System
- **Automatic Fire Detection:** Issues alerts via buzzer, Telegram, or push notifications when fire or explosion signs are detected.
- **Automated Response:** Activates water pump to extinguish fire and deactivates alarm once room temperature normalizes.
- **Manual Control:** Users can manually turn the system on/off locally or remotely via mobile app.

### 🌬️ Air Quality Monitoring System
- **Data Display:** Shows temperature, humidity, light intensity, and CO₂ levels on an LCD screen, Telegram, or software interface.
- **Data Analysis:** Collects and analyzes air quality data weekly, monthly, and annually for indoor air quality assessment.
- **Alert & Recommendations:** Issues alerts when high pollution or other issues are detected and suggests solutions (e.g., turning on lights, closing curtains, opening windows).
- **Remote & Manual Control:** Users can control devices (lights, windows, curtains) remotely or manually based on real-time needs or schedules.
